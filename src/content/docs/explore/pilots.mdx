Two district pilots — in **Ghaziabad** (Uttar Pradesh) and **Dharwad** (Karnataka) — set out to answer a precise question. They did **not** test whether local demand and supply existed. They tested whether that demand and supply could be made **visible** and **actionable**.

The pilots **revealed**, rather than created, demand and supply that was already present.

## Hidden demand, surfaced

When roughly **10% of MSMEs** in each district were Blue Dotted, **over 10,000 local job openings surfaced per district** — many of which had been circulating through word-of-mouth and fragmented postings for months. During the same period, leading national platforms showed **fewer than 100** active listings for the same districts.

> The demand was never absent. It was digitally dark.

## Supply, structured in two weeks

In each of Dharwad and Ghaziabad, **over 15,000 job seekers** surfaced and were structured into discoverable digital signals in just **two weeks** — **20,000+ across the two districts**.

Voice-enabled Blue Dotting reduced intent capture to **2–3 minutes per person**, replacing physical camps, job fairs and intermediary networks that previously took weeks of mobilisation.

## Outcomes

- Placement conversion rose from **around 5%** at conventional placement drives and job fairs to **around 50%** in targeted, digitally enabled drives, where both candidates and jobs are Blue-Dotted before the event.
- Time to discover a relevant candidate fell from **more than 2 weeks to under 2 days**.
- Jobs that once required long commutes — often to another city — were now **within 2 bus stops**.

## The lifecycle in practice

Both districts ran the same [seven-step lifecycle](/core-concepts/blue-dot-lifecycle/) — one cohort of job seekers, one of job providers, reaching them by different routes.

### Dharwad — electrician graduates from ITI Dharwad

ITI Dharwad trains electricians every year, but its graduates had no way of being found by employers beyond word of mouth. The institute became the **aggregator** for the batch.

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| 1. To Become a Blue Dot | The district identified ITI Dharwad as the aggregator with direct reach to a graduating batch of electricians — real skills, no digital visibility |
| 2. Route in | **Assisted** — the institute already held student records |
| 3. Data collection | **Bulk upload** of the whole batch at once, rather than student by student |
| 4. Profile completion | Each graduate reached by **AI Voice Agent** — a short call, at a fraction of the cost of a manual survey, confirming role preferences, location and trade skills. The webapp stayed available for students who preferred it |
| 5. Blue Dot checks | Each profile checked automatically for structured, complete, consented |
| 6. Blue Dot Verified | ITI Dharwad reviewed and confirmed the batch on the Aggregator DPG |
| 7. Live Blue Dot | Each electrician a live, discoverable job seeker — local manufacturers could find exactly this skill set for the first time, instead of relying on informal referrals |

### Ghaziabad — an MSME association converting job openings into Blue Dots

Ghaziabad's manufacturing MSMEs had jobs to fill, often circulating only by word of mouth for months. A local MSME association took on the **aggregator** role for its member businesses.

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| 1. To Become a Blue Dot | The Ghaziabad administration, with UPSDM and the DIC, identified the MSME association as best placed to reach its manufacturing members |
| 2. Route in | **Assisted** — an onboarding drive at a member gathering, since many owners would not have registered on their own |
| 3. Data collection | An **inbound campaign** — members scanned a shared QR code and registered their open roles on the spot |
| 4. Profile completion | Each business completed its listing — role, requirements, location — through the AI Voice Agent or the webapp |
| 5. Blue Dot checks | Each listing checked automatically for structured, complete, consented |
| 6. Blue Dot Verified | The association reviewed and confirmed new listings on the Aggregator DPG |
| 7. Live Blue Dot | Each opening a live, discoverable Blue Dot |

Across the district, this conversion took the pilot from **under 100 jobs visible on national platforms to over 10,000 local openings** surfaced from under 10% of MSMEs — with discovery time falling from over two weeks to under two days.

## Named proof

> Shri Abhinav Gopal, IAS, Chief Development Officer, Ghaziabad, outlined how job and candidate aggregators come together to generate Blue Dots across the district through a **Jobs Facilitation Centre (JFC)**.

In **Dharwad**, manufacturers were convened by a local MSME association to convert their job openings into Blue Dots, and electrician graduates from **ITI Dharwad** were made digitally discoverable.

## Ecosystem uptake

A range of organisations are already leveraging Blue Dots — placement and skilling organisations sharpening their targeting, assessment providers, and local operators scaling deployment. Named participants from the pilots include:

- Head Held High
- Recex
- JobsUFL
- TRRAIN
- Proof Of Skill
- Digital Labour Chowk

## Momentum

Both districts are expanding the approach internally. **Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka are scaling it to multiple districts across each state in the coming year.**
