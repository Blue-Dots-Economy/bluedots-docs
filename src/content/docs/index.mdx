## What Blue Dots does

In most districts, people who need something live close to people who offer it. Most of them are not listed anywhere online, so they never find each other. Blue Dots lets each of them publish a **blue dot**: a short listing that says what they need or offer, placed where they are, the way a map app shows your own location as a blue dot. People nearby who can help see the blue dot and ask to connect.

For example:

- **Blue Dot (jobs):** A young person registers by phone call in their own language. A nearby employer with a vacancy sees the profile and sends an interview invite. The young person accepts before their number is shared.
- **Yellow Dot (education):** A Class 12 commerce student asks for help with accounts. A tutor who teaches Class 12 commerce and will travel up to 5 km sends a connect request. The student accepts.
- **Purple Dot (persons with disabilities):** A person with a locomotor disability asks for a wheelchair and for help finding work. An NGO that gives devices and a training centre in the same city see the request and send a connect request.

Local organisations help people sign up and check their details. These include skilling centres and Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs), schools and education NGOs, disabled persons' organisations (DPOs), self-help groups (SHGs), farmer producer organisations (FPOs), tourism bodies, trade associations and government departments.

## The seven domains

Each colour names a domain (one sector, such as jobs or education), and all of them run on the same software. Blue (jobs) is live and has been piloted. Yellow, Purple and Orange each have a published minimum standard, the list of fields every blue dot in that domain must have; Pink, Green and Brown are possible future domains. Read more in [The Dots Family](/explore/beyond-livelihoods/).

## How it is built

<CardGrid>
  <FeatureCard title="Free and open source" icon="globe" color="green">
    Blue Dots is two open Digital Public Goods (DPGs), **Signals** and **Aggregator**.
    Any government department or organisation can deploy and change them.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="One set of software for every sector" icon="layers" color="orange">
    Each domain has its own data rules, written as a schema. Adding a sector means
    writing a new schema. See [the schema-driven model](/get-started/architecture/schema-driven-model/).
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Sign up by phone call" icon="mic" color="purple">
    People can create a blue dot through a voice call in their own language, or in the app.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Tested in the field" icon="map-pin" color="blue">
    The jobs domain has run in pilots in Ghaziabad and Dharwad.
    See [the pilot results](/explore/pilots/).
  </FeatureCard>
</CardGrid>
