Blue Dots helps people and organisations near each other find each other. This section explains how the software does that, in three layers:

1. **Concepts** — the words, the steps a participant goes through, and the two DPGs: [Signals](/core-concepts/signals/), [The Blue Dot Lifecycle](/core-concepts/blue-dot-lifecycle/), [Participant Profiles](/core-concepts/participant-profiles/), [Aggregators](/core-concepts/aggregators/), [Domains, Participant Types & Instances](/core-concepts/networks-domains-instances/), [Items, Actions & Events](/core-concepts/items-actions-events/) and the [Glossary](/core-concepts/glossary/).
2. **Architecture** — how the system is put together: [High-Level Architecture](/core-concepts/architecture/high-level-architecture/), the [Signals DPG](/core-concepts/architecture/signals-dpg/) and [Aggregator DPG](/core-concepts/architecture/aggregator-dpg/), the [Data Model](/core-concepts/architecture/data-model/), [Identity & Auth](/core-concepts/architecture/identity-and-auth/), the [Keycloak Realm Reference](/core-concepts/architecture/keycloak-realm/) and [Infrastructure & Deployment](/core-concepts/architecture/infrastructure/).
3. **Technical Documentation** — implementation detail: the [Technical Overview](/core-concepts/technical/overview/), [Schema-Driven Model](/core-concepts/technical/schema-driven-model/), [Read & Write Paths](/core-concepts/technical/read-write-paths/), and the [Tech Stack](/core-concepts/technical/tech-stack/).

## The mental model in one paragraph

A *blue dot* is a short listing that shows a person, organisation or service where they are, like the blue dot that marks you on a map app. A *domain* is one sector and its set of rules, written in a schema: for example the jobs domain (`blue_dot`) or the education domain (`yellow_dot`). A *participant type* is a role inside that domain (for example `seeker` in the jobs domain, or `student` in the education domain). An *instance* is one running deployment of the Signals API serving one or more participant types. Inside an instance, every record is an *item* — a versioned, schema-typed object such as `profile_1.0`. Interactions between items are *actions*, and their structured results are *events*. The Aggregator DPG sits on top of this, helping organisations create items (signals) on behalf of the participants they onboard.

These terms mean the same thing in the product, the docs and the code.
