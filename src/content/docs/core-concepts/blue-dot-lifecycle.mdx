Every participant — a job seeker, a job provider, or a service provider — goes through the **same seven steps** to become a *Blue Dot*: a live, verified, discoverable entry in the system.

Each step is either done **by an entity** (an aggregator, acting on the participant's behalf) or **by the participant themselves**. The DPGs needed for every step already exist and are ready to configure — an adaptor configures what is already built rather than building it.

:::note[Two lifecycles, don't confuse them]
This page describes the **participant** journey — how a person or organisation becomes a Blue Dot. The **signal** lifecycle — how a stored item is discovered, matched and acted on — is a different thing, covered in [From signal to match](/overview/the-blue-dots-approach/#from-signal-to-match) and [Read & Write Paths](/core-concepts/technical/read-write-paths/).
:::

## Who does what

| Step | Done by | Where it happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. To Become a Blue Dot | Adaptor | Off-platform — a scoping and convening decision |
| 2. Choose the route in | Adaptor or field partner | Configuration, per campaign or geography |
| 3. Data collection | Aggregator, on the participant's behalf | Aggregator DPG |
| 4. Completing the profile | The participant | AI Voice Agent or Blue Dot Webapp → Signals DPG |
| 5. Verification checks | The system | Signals DPG |
| 6. Blue Dot Verified | Adaptor or aggregator — **a person** | Aggregator DPG |
| 7. Live Blue Dot | The network | Signals DPG (+ voice bot) |

---

## 1. To Become a Blue Dot

The starting point for any participant type: a job seeker looking for work, a job provider with a vacancy, or a service provider offering support such as counselling, upskilling, assessment or placement help.

The work at this step is **identification** — who should become a Blue Dot, and on what criteria. The adaptor decides the cohort, and identifies the **aggregator**: the local field partner or organisation with reach and trust on the ground who can actually get to them.

See [Aggregators](/core-concepts/aggregators/) for who qualifies, and [Activating Blue Dots in a District](/guides/district-activation/) for how a district picks its first use case.

## 2. Choose the route in

The adaptor (or its field partner) chooses how a given group will be onboarded:

- **Assisted** — the adaptor or field team brings the participant in on their behalf.
- **Self** — the participant starts the process on their own.

Both routes are supported out of the box. This is a **configuration choice made per campaign or geography**, not a technical build.

In the Aggregator DPG this is expressed as a per-link `registration_mode`, declared under `aggregator.registration_modes` in `aggregator.config.yaml`. Each mode resolves to a submission shape — `account_only` (create the account now, complete the profile later) or `account_and_profile` (capture everything in one pass). The database column is just a key; the config is the single source of truth.

## 3. Data collection (assisted route)

Where onboarding is assisted, the adaptor gathers participant data through whichever method fits the context. All four are **ready-made intake methods** on the Aggregator DPG — there is no need to build a separate tool for each.

| Intake method | What it looks like | What it uses |
|---|---|---|
| **Offline camp** | Data collected in person, e.g. at a job fair | Aggregator portal, assisted entry |
| **Bulk upload** | An existing list (CSV, database) imported in one go | CSV upload → async background worker → bulk-create into Signals |
| **Outbound campaign** | The adaptor, via the aggregator, reaches out proactively over email or a voice bot | Notifications + voice DPG |
| **Inbound campaign** | The participant scans a QR code shared by the aggregator and starts on their own | Shareable registration links, with roll-up metrics |

## 4. Completing the profile (self route)

Whether the participant arrived through assisted intake or came in on their own, the profile is completed through one of two self-service channels:

- **AI Voice Agent (primary)** — a call-based option, best suited to participants who prefer speaking over typing. The prompts driving it are themselves published as DPGs. See [AI Voice Agent Prompts](/guides/voice-ai-prompts/).
- **Blue Dot Webapp (fallback)** — a self-serve web app built on the Signals DPG.

Both channels write into the **same underlying profile**, handled by the Signals DPG, which already has the participant type — Job Seeker, Job Provider or Service Provider — defined and ready to use. See [Participant Profiles](/core-concepts/participant-profiles/).

## 5. Verification checks

Before a profile can go live, three checks are applied:

1. **Structured** — the data is in the shape the schema expects.
2. **Completed** — all required fields are filled in.
3. **Consented** — consent has been captured.

These are built into the system. Nothing here needs to be custom-built by the adaptor. Because the checks run against the [item schema](/core-concepts/technical/schema-driven-model/), changing what "structured" and "completed" mean is a schema edit, not a code change.

## 6. Blue Dot Verified

A final review by the adaptor or the aggregator, confirming the profile is genuine and ready to go live.

:::tip[The one human step, by design]
Software runs the checks; **a person confirms the outcome.** This is the only human-in-the-loop step in the journey, and it is deliberate — it is where an aggregator's ground-level trust is attached to the Blue Dot.
:::

The review happens on the Aggregator DPG, against the fields that can be verified. The adaptor checks with the aggregator that the details have been verified.

## 7. Live Blue Dot

The participant is now live: visible to others in the system and eligible to be matched — a job seeker surfaced to employers, a vacancy surfaced to job seekers, or a service provider discoverable by either.

From here, two capabilities are available across the Signals DPG and the voice bot:

- **Discover** — being found by, and finding, relevant matches.
- **Connect** — reaching out to, or being reached by, a match.

:::caution[Contact details are not public]
**Personal contact details are shared only once both parties agree to connect.** Discovery surfaces that a relevant match exists; it does not hand over phone numbers. See [Consent is foundational](/core-concepts/signals/#consent-is-foundational).
:::
